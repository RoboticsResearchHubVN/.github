<h1 align="center">Robotics Research Hub Vietnam</h1>

<p align="center">
  <b>Open research on humanoid and legged robot learning, from Vietnam.</b>
</p>

---

**Robotics Research Hub Vietnam** builds and shares open-source tools for training controllers for humanoid and legged robots.
Our work centers on fast, GPU-native robot learning: differentiable world models, adversarial motion priors, and
reinforcement learning that runs end-to-end on a single GPU with JAX and MuJoCo Warp. We aim to make state-of-the-art
robot learning reproducible and affordable for students and researchers in Vietnam and beyond.

## Research focus

- **Humanoid locomotion**: natural walking, running and get-up behaviors for platforms such as the Unitree G1
- **Robot learning**: reinforcement learning, imitation from human motion, and adversarial motion priors (AMP)
- **Differentiable simulation and world models**: policy optimization with analytic gradients through learned dynamics
- **Efficient training**: fully compiled pipelines that train in minutes on one consumer GPU
